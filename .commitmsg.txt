feat(mar-889): a versioned span side channel from agent to store

Spans travel on their own message, their own runner route and their own
schema, and telemetry v1 is untouched. `contract.lock.json` fingerprints
the frozen v1 schemas and third parties validate against it, so an eighth
event type would be a breaking change to a contract DASH does not own both
ends of -- and `listRuns` derives a run's status from its events, so a
span arriving as an event would be a description of the work able to
change what DASH says the work was.

- `contracts/run-span.schema.json` (trace_version 1) and an example.
- `runner/protocol.ts` parses `{ type: "trace", span }`; `supervisor.ts`
  gets its own bounded buffer and `drainSpans`; `server.ts` answers
  `POST /traces/drain`.
- `lib/agent-dom/evidence.ts` drains it beside telemetry and artifacts. A
  runner built before this route answers 404, which reads as "no spans"
  rather than as a failure.
- `lib/db.ts` migration index 38 (user_version 39): `run_spans` and
  `run_span_drops`. `lib/store.ts` `ingestSpans` upserts on
  (agent, run_id, span_id) -- a span is written on open and on close --
  with an attribute allowlist, a 300-character error bound and the same
  credential filter `tests/contracts.test.ts` applies to every example.
